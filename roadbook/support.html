RoadBook — Support

RoadBook records your car journeys and turns them into a mileage log and route posters. Everything stays on your iPhone, so there is no account to sign in to and nothing to reset on a server.

Need a hand? Email manostudio2020@gmail.com. The quickest way is Settings → Contact Us in the app, which fills in the app version for you. If you write from somewhere else, it helps to include the version (shown at the top of Settings → User Guide) and your iPhone model.

The User Guide in Settings explains how each part of the app works. The answers below cover the questions people ask most.

Recording drives

How do I record a drive?

Tap the dial on the Drive screen to start, and tap it again to finish. That works with the app in your pocket or the phone locked, and it is free.

Automatic recording (part of RoadBook Pro) starts and ends drives without you opening the app. Turn it on in Settings. It needs Location set to Always, and works better with Motion & Fitness allowed.

A drive was not recorded, or started late

iOS decides when to wake RoadBook, and without help it often does so a few hundred metres into a drive. A very short drive can be missed altogether.

To record from the first metre, let the car tell RoadBook a drive is coming. In the Shortcuts app, add an automation for when your car's Bluetooth connects, or for when CarPlay connects, choose Run Immediately, and pick RoadBook → Watch for a Drive. RoadBook then starts listening as you start the engine or plug in. Settings → Car Automations has the steps.

If drives are not being recorded at all, check:

  • Automatic recording is switched on in Settings.
  • Location is set to Always (Settings shows it, and the row opens RoadBook's page in the iPhone Settings app).
  • RoadBook Pro is active. If a subscription or free week has ended, the Drive screen and Settings both say that automatic recording has stopped.

When in doubt, tap the dial. A drive recorded by hand is never missed.

Why do I see the location indicator when I am not driving?

iOS sometimes wakes RoadBook when you have not moved, and RoadBook then uses GPS for a few minutes to check whether a drive has started. The indicator goes off again on its own. Switching automatic recording off stops this completely.

I was a passenger and RoadBook recorded the drive

RoadBook cannot tell who is driving. Delete the drive on the Trips tab: swipe it to the left, or use Select.

If you finish or discard a recording while the car is moving, RoadBook takes that as "not this drive" and starts nothing on its own until it sees the car stop or you get out. If you then take the wheel without getting out, tap the dial to record your drive.

For a longer ride, you can switch automatic recording off in Settings. Remember to switch it back on afterwards.

The distance differs from my odometer

Distances come from GPS and may differ a little from your car's odometer. Where recording stopped and started again, the gap is left out rather than estimated, so RoadBook never counts distance it did not measure. Check the figures before you submit a claim.

The mileage log

How do I make a mileage report?

Open the Reports tab (the row at the top of the Trips list goes there too). Choose a period, and narrow it by trip type, purpose or car if you need to. Then export a PDF to hand in, listing every trip, or a CSV spreadsheet for your accountant.

To report particular drives, tap Select on the Trips tab, choose them, and pick Create Report from the menu. You can also open a single drive and use Create Report from its menu.

What is the difference between a trip's type and its purpose?

The type is the kind of driving a claim separates: Private, Business, Commute, or one you add yourself. The purpose is why you made the drive, in your own words, such as "client visit". A claim form usually asks for both.

To sort a month of driving in one go, tap Select on the Trips tab, choose the drives, and set the type and the purpose from the menu.

Can I see a report I sent earlier?

Yes. Every report you share from the Reports tab is kept under Shared Reports, exactly as it was sent, even if you have changed the trips since. Tap one to open it, and share it again from there. Making the same report again today might not give the same result, so this is the copy to check when someone asks what you claimed.

Does RoadBook calculate my deduction?

No. RoadBook gives you accurate distances and never applies a mileage rate, because rates differ by country and change every year. Your accountant or tax form applies the rate to the distance.

Your data

Is my driving uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no account and no server, and nothing you record is sent to us. A few things go to Apple, such as the start and end of each drive for its place names, and map tiles. The Privacy Policy lists all of them.

How do I back up my trips, or move them to a new iPhone?

Your trips are included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud or a computer) and move with them to a new phone.

For a copy you control, use Settings → Data → Back Up All Trips. That writes one GPX file with every drive — its trip type, purpose and car — plus your trip types, your cars with their number plates, and your name. On the new phone, tap Import on the Trips tab and choose the file: the drives come back, and any trip type, car or name the phone does not have yet is added. Nothing already on the phone is overwritten. The file holds your plates and your name, so keep it private. Backing up is free and always will be.

To send a few drives to someone else, use Export GPX from Select on the Trips tab instead. That file leaves out your plates, your name and your setup.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Erase All Driving Data removes every trip, poster, shared report, vehicle and your name in one go. It cannot be undone, so tap Back Up All Trips just above it first if you want a copy.

RoadBook Pro

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Free: recording drives by hand, the full trip list and each trip's details, the route replay, GPX export and import, and the Midnight poster style.

Pro adds automatic recording, the other seven poster styles, poster videos, and the Reports tab: the totals and the PDF and CSV mileage reports.

Pro is a yearly subscription, which can start with a free week, or a one-off lifetime purchase. Neither needs an account.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing you have recorded is ever locked. Your trips, posters and GPX export stay. If you have ever paid for Pro, the Reports tab stays open too, so your mileage log can still be reported. What stops is automatic recording, the Pro poster styles and video. Buying Pro again turns automatic recording back on where you left it.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Tap the RoadBook Pro row at the top of Settings, or go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To cancel a free week before it charges, do it at least a day before the week ends. If you allow notifications when you start the week, RoadBook reminds you two days before it ends.

I bought Pro, but the app does not show it

Open Settings → See plans and tap Restore. RoadBook asks Apple for your purchases again. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
