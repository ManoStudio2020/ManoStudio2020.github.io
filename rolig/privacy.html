Privacy Policy

Rolig · Last updated October 15, 2026

Rolig (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a standalone ambient sound app for sleep and relaxation. We designed it to respect your privacy: the app has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or precise location, and the app contains no advertising.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone input. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences — favorited sounds, saved mixes, per-sound volumes, your last mix, and your colour theme — are stored only on your device. This data never leaves your phone and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Rolig Pro is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app checks your purchase on your device using Apple’s StoreKit; we do not use any third-party purchase service and never receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Network use

All sounds are bundled in the app and play fully offline. The app uses the network only to reach Apple’s App Store — to show the Rolig Pro price, and when you buy it or tap “Restore Purchases”.

Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can remove all locally stored preferences at any time by deleting the app. Your purchase history is held by Apple and can be viewed in your Apple ID account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent version.
